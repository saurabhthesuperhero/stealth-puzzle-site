Sneak past the guards, one move at a time.

Rascal the raccoon is a cat-burglar with a soft step and a big appetite for shiny things. In each tiny diorama heist you move one step, then every guard takes its turn: the bulldog stands watch, the goose patrols, the owl turns its head, the bear dozes, the hound sniffs you out and the cameras sweep. Every guard shows where it looks now and where it looks next, so every heist is a fair logic puzzle.

Turn-based stealth puzzles across the museum, the mansion, the bank vault, the night train and the lighthouse, plus a free daily heist.
