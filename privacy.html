Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Applies to the Android game Masked Rascal: Stealth Puzzle (package com.saikaizen.stealthpuzzle), made by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us").

In short: there is no account and no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone. We show ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID (with your consent where the law requires it). We use Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to find crashes. Purchases go through Google Play. We do not sell your personal data, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

1. What stays on your device

Levels solved, stars, hints, outfits, the daily streak and your settings are saved on your phone. Android may include them in your own device backup (Google's backup service) so progress survives a reinstall. We do not run a server and do not receive this data.

3. Ads and your consent choices

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, see a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
  • Buying "Remove Ads" stops ads between levels and banners. Reward videos you choose to watch stay available.

4. Notifications and sharing

If you allow notifications, the game schedules at most one reminder a day on your phone (for example for the daily heist). They are created on the device, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in Settings. Share cards are drawn on your phone and passed to the app you pick in the system share sheet; we do not see them.

5. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads, analytics, purchases), notifications (reminders, asked only after you win a level), run at startup (to restore scheduled reminders after a reboot), vibrate (haptics), and the advertising ID permission (ads). The game does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

6. Your rights

Because there is no account, we cannot link analytics or crash data to your name. You can still ask us to access or delete data: email us with the subject "Privacy request". Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Our legal bases under the GDPR are your consent (personalized ads) and our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it (analytics, crash reports).

We do not sell personal data for money. Ad partners may use the advertising ID for personalized ads where you allow it. Where a US state law calls this a "sale" or "sharing", you can opt out through Settings > Privacy options in the game or by turning off ad personalization on your device.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Progress on your phone is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for a limited period under its retention settings, and crash reports for about 90 days. Uninstalling the game stops all collection. We hold no account data on any server.

8. Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
