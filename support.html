Support

Email superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, Android version, the game version (Android Settings > Apps > Masked Rascal) and the level number. We usually reply within 2 working days.

How do I play?

Tap a glowing ring to step there. After every move each guard takes its turn. Red squares are where a guard looks now, so never step into them. Dashed orange squares show where a guard will look after your move. Step onto a guard from outside its view to tie it up (hounds and cameras can't be tied up). Reach the exit to escape.

How do I get 3 stars?

Escape for the first star. The other two are shown on the level card, for example finish within par moves, grab every gem, or tie up no one. Your best result for each star is kept, so you can earn them on different tries.

I'm stuck on a level

Use Undo (free undos on every try), Restart, or a Hint, which shows the next safe move. Every level is checked by a solver, so every level and every star can be done.

Restore my purchases

Open Settings and tap "Restore" while signed in to the same Google account you bought with. Remove Ads, the Grand Heist pack and the bundle come back on a new phone too.

Ads and privacy choices

Change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy options (shown in regions where consent applies). "Remove Ads" stops ads between levels and banners; reward videos stay optional.

Reminders

Turn the daily reminder on or off in Settings. If you denied the notification permission, you can allow it again in Android Settings > Apps > Masked Rascal > Notifications.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your phone and included in your Android backup, so it usually comes back after a reinstall on the same Google account. Clearing the game's data deletes it.

Delete my data

The game has no account. Uninstall the game or clear its data to remove everything on your phone. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data, email us with the subject "Privacy request". Details are in the Privacy Policy.
